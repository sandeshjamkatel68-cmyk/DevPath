Privacy Policy for DevPath

Last updated: July 3, 2026

DevPath ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile learning app that teaches web development. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

The short version

DevPath does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data with anyone. Everything the app knows about you stays on your device.

What information the app stores

DevPath stores the following information locally on your device only, using standard Android storage (Room database and DataStore preferences):

  • The name you enter when you first open the app (used only to personalize your Profile screen).
  • Your lesson, quiz, and project progress (which lessons you've completed, quiz scores, streaks, XP, and level).
  • Bookmarks and notes you create while studying.

None of this information ever leaves your device. DevPath has no backend server, no user accounts, and no login — there is nothing to send your data to.

What the app does not do

  • No internet access is used or required. All lesson content is bundled inside the app itself.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are included.
  • No advertising SDKs are included.
  • No third-party data sharing of any kind, because no data leaves the device in the first place.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No contacts, camera, microphone, or other sensitive permissions are requested.

Data deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you can delete everything DevPath knows about you at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the app, or
  • Clearing the app's storage from Android Settings → Apps → DevPath → Storage & cache → Clear storage.

Either action permanently and immediately erases your name, progress, and any notes/bookmarks — there is no copy anywhere else.

Children's privacy

DevPath does not knowingly collect any personal information, and since no data ever leaves the device, there is no data collection risk for users of any age, including children.

Changes to this policy

If DevPath's data handling ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated first, and the update will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above before that feature ships.
